# Waymark — notes for Claude

Waymark is a travel-log web app published as a claude.ai artifact. The owner (Olivier) is beta-testing it on a trip and may ask for changes from a phone.

## Where things live
- **Live app (source of truth):** https://claude.ai/artifact/VoEoHtepSYciZXkd8Uvspo
- **Business plan page:** https://claude.ai/artifact/T3Ut4zHmKMGTgJujHvNcjo
- `src/app.template.html` — app source. `__WORLD__` is replaced by `src/countries-50m.json` (world-atlas 50m TopoJSON, sharp enough for coastlines at city zoom) at build time.
- `src/build.py` — `python3 src/build.py` writes `waymark.html` at the repo root, the file that gets published.
- `src/playbook.html` — source of the business plan page.
- `src/video-test.html` — one-off phone test for recording the route as video (published separately: https://claude.ai/artifact/XyaNs7QxB6NUPxNxx8KP5v, capability `downloads`).

## How to change the app
1. **Read the live artifact first** (Artifact tool, `action: "read"`, the URL above). If it differs from `src/app.template.html`, the live version wins; bring the repo up to date before editing.
2. Edit `src/app.template.html`, run `python3 src/build.py`.
3. Republish **to the same URL**: Artifact tool, `file_path: waymark.html`, `url: https://claude.ai/artifact/VoEoHtepSYciZXkd8Uvspo`. Omit `capabilities` so the stored ones carry over (db, assets, sample, downloads, mcp/Strava). Omit `icon`.
4. Commit the change to this repo with a short message.

## Never
- Don't write to, reset or reseed the database unless the owner asks. Trips, places, photos and expenses are real data.
- Don't delete uploaded photos (assets).
- Don't change the artifact's sharing or capabilities without asking.

## Data model (artifact `db` collections)
- `trips`: name, startDate, endDate, currency, notes, past (bool: added from memory via "Add past trips"), steps {YYYY-MM-DD: count} (👟 per day), people [{name, email, phone}] ("Who's going" in the trip form; names feed "Paid by"; contacts never go on a share)
- `stops` (cities): tripId, city, country, countryA2, countryN3, lat, lon, arrive, depart, home (bool, for the home city), photoIds[]
- `legs`: tripId, mode (plane|car|train|bus|ferry|walk), fromId, toId, date, time (departure, optional HH:MM), arrTime (arrival, optional HH:MM; the form shows "⏱ 1h10 on the way" and saves durationS from the two, an earlier arrival meaning the next day; GPS/Timeline imports fill it from start + duration; Route shows "19:05 → 20:15 · 1h10"), km, kmEstimated, cost, currency, paidBy (shown in the leg form when there's a cost), notes; a drive recorded with a GPS app also carries track [[lat,lon],…] (≤400 pts) and durationS, and is drawn along its road (`legTrack`) on the map, the share card and the video
- `places` (shown as "activities"; meals are places too): tripId, stopId, name, category (meals: breakfast|lunch|dinner|coffee|drinks, picked from the time/name by `mealFor`; things to do: citytour ("City visit" 🏙️), museum, attraction, hike, run, walk, ride, hotel, nightlife, shopping, other; old food|restaurant still read as meals), date, time (optional HH:MM), private (bool: "🔒 Keep off shares", starts unticked on every share), status (want|done), rating 0–5, cost, currency, notes, photoIds[], lat, lon (pin on the trip map; from photo GPS or Claude); imported runs/rides/hikes also carry track [[lat,lon],…] (≤400 pts), distanceKm, movingS, elevM, sport, stravaId
- `expenses`: tripId, label, amount, currency, category, date, time (optional HH:MM), city, stopId (set when city matches a trip stop), paidBy, notes, photoIds[] (receipt photos; "Scan" reads them with `sample` images)
- `stays` (nights): tripId, stopId, city, name, kind (hotel|airbnb|hostel|camping|friends|other), checkIn, checkOut (nights = days between), nights, cost (total, optional), currency, pricePerNight, platform (Booking.com|Airbnb|Expedia|Hotels.com|Vrbo|Hostelworld|Direct|Other|''), bookingRef, address, lat, lon, rating 0–5, paidBy (when there's a cost), notes, photoIds[]. Shown at the end of each night's day in Days ("🛏 Where did you sleep?" when a night has none), on the city page, as H pins on the map, and under Lodging in the budget. Fields are kept structured so stays (and later restaurants/activities) can be analysed across trips.
- Photos are artifact assets, shown via `/_blob/<id>`.
- Icons: `CAT_ICON` (activity/meal types), `MODE_EMOJI` (legs: ✈️ 🚗 🚆 🚌 ⛴️ 🚶) and `EXP_ICON` (expense categories; Transport/Other expenses get a specific icon from their label via `RIDE_ICONS`: flight, train, taxi, fuel, toll, parking, bus, ferry, bike, car) are emoji used in Days, the city page, the budget, the share image and video cards. The budget groups meal costs under "Meals" and splits transport so a flight reads as a flight (`LEG_CAT` for leg costs, `RIDE_CAT` for Transport expenses: Flights, Trains, Car, Taxis, Fuel, Tolls, Parking, Buses, Boats). The trip map shows a mode badge (✈️ 🚗 …) on each leg; a flight to/from home gets it ~12 km from the trip end so it's on screen. A Food & drink expense can be turned into a meal from its sheet ("Make it a meal" creates the place, moves the photos, deletes the expense); the reverse is "💳 Turn into an expense" in the activity/meal form; meals/activities, legs and stays with a price also have "Paid by" (`paidBy`), all counted in Who paid and the Excel export (`payers`/`lastPayer` look across expenses, places, legs and stays); a restaurant receipt read from a photo becomes a meal. Meals are added from each day's ＋ menu.

## Runtime
- Plain HTML/JS, no build tooling beyond `build.py`. d3 7.9.0 and topojson 3.0.2 load from cdnjs.
- Capabilities used: `db` (data), `assets` (photos), `sample` (Claude for city/activity coordinates, reading photos and receipts, Top 10), `downloads` (share image), `mcp` (the owner's Strava connector, read-only tools `list_activities` and `get_activity_streams`; added at the owner's request).
- Strava import (Days → From Strava): reads tool input schemas at runtime with `describeTool`, falls back to Claude to normalize an unfamiliar payload. GPX import (Days → GPX file) is parsed in the page.
- When republishing with `capabilities`, pass the full set: {db:{}, assets:{}, sample:{}, downloads:{}, mcp:{servers:[{server:"Strava", tools:["list_activities","get_activity_streams"]}]}}. Omitting `capabilities` keeps the stored ones, which is the normal case.
- Trip page, photo-first: under the dates, a big "📷 Add photos" (`photoBatch`: reads EXIF, groups photos taken within 40 min and ~1 km into one moment (≤6 each), uploads, runs `readPhoto` per moment and `whereIs` for GPS, then `mergeWander` joins consecutive sightseeing moments (attraction/citytour/walk/other/shopping) in the same city on the same day, less than 3 h apart, into one "<City> city visit" with all the photos and the stops' names in notes — meals, receipts, stays and specific activities stay separate, and the card's "Split into N" restores the parts; if reading several photos fails, `readPhoto` retries with the first one; an unnamed moment takes the first part of its `whereIs` address; then opens `reviewSheet`) and a "Quick add" box (`quickAdd`: free text or iPhone keyboard dictation → Claude returns one or more meals/activities/expenses → same `reviewSheet`). `reviewSheet` shows one editable card per entry (type chips, name, category, city, date, time, price, keep/skip) and "Save all" creates places/expenses/stays (new towns via `ensureStop`); skipped photos are released with `forgetAsset`. Strava, GPX, Stay and Expense live under "More ways to add".
- Budget "⬇ Excel" (`exportExpenses`): an .xlsx built in the page (`xlsx`/`zipStore`, no library) with sheets Expenses (every cost: legs, meals/activities, expenses, stays — date, time, type, category, description, city, amount, currency, paid by, notes), By category (per currency, with totals) and Who paid; saved via `downloads`.
- Active KPI (`activeHtml`, under the stats strip): a 👟 Steps box first when the trip has steps (total, a-day average, ≈ km on foot at 0.75 m a step; tap opens `stepsSheet`), then km, count and moving time per sport (`SPORTS`: run, ride, hike, walk) from done activities' `distanceKm`/`movingS`; only sports the trip has appear, so each traveller sees their own mix. The activity form shows Distance km and Time (h:mm) for those categories (`#pSportF`); Strava/GPX imports fill them.
- Look (owner asked for a less bland app; step 1 of a visual refresh): trip page opens with a photo cover (`tripCover`: the photo picked in `coverSheet` (🖼 on the cover, saved as trip `coverId`), else the best-rated non-private activity photo, meals last), with the name and dates over it; the trips list cards use the same cover with the On the road / Upcoming pill on it. In Days each activity's photos are a scrolling strip under its line (`.ph-strip`, up to 8, first one wider) instead of 30 px thumbnails. Ideas still to come if the owner wants: colour per kind of entry, a darker stats band, calmer borders/helper text.
- Trip page layout: dates, the stats strip and Active KPIs, then one compact add row (📷 button · Quick add box · Add; owner asked for the numbers first and a smaller photo button), then a sticky section bar (`TRIP_SECS`: Days · To do · Map · Route · Cities · Budget) showing one section at a time (`showSec`; last choice kept in `R.sec` and localStorage `wm.sec`; an orange dot on Route when `missingLegs` has suggestions). `go()` remembers each screen's scroll position (`scrollMem`), and going back (city → trip → list) returns to it instead of the top.
- To do section (owner's request: recommended restaurants, a dish, a museum): to-dos are `places` with status `want` and `todo:true` (undated wants from Top 10 show there too). Typed/dictated text goes through `readTodos` (Claude splits it into name, category, city, notes such as who recommended it) → `addTodos`; "＋ With details" opens `placeSheet` with `opts.todo` (no date, no time, Want to go). A to-do has no date and, if its town isn't on the trip yet, no stop: the town is kept as text in `city` (`todoCity`). Open items are grouped by town; ticking (`tickTodo`) marks it done today (or its planned date if earlier) at now, files it under its town (`ensureStop` for a text town) or where you were then, so it appears in Days; un-ticking puts it back. Each row has 📷 to add photos straight to it.
- Trip page Map section: zoomable map (pinch with two fingers or +/−, tap a pin, ▶ replays the route); Days section: the timeline. While a trip is under way, opening it from the list scrolls Days to today (`toToday`), and days before today fold to one line (cities, "N things · km"; tap the date to open, kept in `openDays` for the session). Each day with something logged has a share button (`data-dayshare` → `shareSheet(t,{from:d,to:d})`) next to its ＋ (`dayMenu`): Photos, Meal, Activity, Expense, Drive/flight, Drive from GPS file, Where we slept, Steps, Share this day, all dated to that day. "From photo" reads a picture's EXIF date/GPS and asks Claude what it shows; a receipt becomes an expense, anything else an activity.
- Day header towns (`dayCities`) come from what happened that day: where you woke up (`morningStop`: the stay covering the night before, else a planned itinerary stop that starts after the last earlier leg, else where that leg arrived), the day's legs, activities, expenses and the night's stay; an empty past day shows the morning town, an empty future day only stops planned to start that day. Stored stop arrive/depart ranges are not trusted for this (an old leg edit can leave them stretched). `stopOnDate(tid,d,time)` (the fallback city for new entries) first takes the destination of the day's last leg that had arrived by that time, then `morningStop` (an untowned 16:03 lunch is filed where you were then, not where you woke up — that once produced a phantom Capbreton → Serres-Castet drive); an activity with a pin but no town goes to the trip town within 15 km of the pin. `ensureStop`'s `at` (photo/pin coordinates) wins when the geocode lands >5 km away (30 km for Timeline ends). A new leg defaults to today during the trip and starts from `morningStop`.
- Days order (dayOrder): a manual order wins once set (press and hold ⠿ for ~0.35 s, then drag; a quick swipe over the handle just scrolls. Dragging writes `dayPos` 10,20,… on that day's legs/places/expenses; changing an item's date clears it; unpositioned items slot in after their automatic neighbour). Otherwise: times win when known; untimed items go with their city between the legs (after the leg arriving there, before the leg leaving); items with no city go last. Time is pre-filled from photo EXIF/receipt (the first added photo with EXIF sets date and time, replacing the "now" default unless the owner typed a time), Strava/GPX start, or now when logging on the same day. Every Days line has a time slot (the time, or "+ time") that opens the phone's time wheel and saves `time` directly.
- Expenses have the same Location field via `locFieldHtml`/`bindLoc` (receipt photo GPS → pin + `whereIs` text; saved as address/lat/lon); stays fill the address and pin from a photo's GPS when empty, and their City is free text too (empty for a new stay, set from the photo's or booking's own town, new towns added with `ensureStop`; if a town can't be placed the entry is filed under that day's city instead of failing). The main "From photo" flow carries the GPS into whichever form it opens.
- Activity/meal Location field (`pLoc`, saved as `address`): a photo's EXIF GPS pins it and `whereIs` asks Claude what's there from the coordinates alone (no trip-city hint, which made it guess a nearby trip place) to fill the text; City is a free-text field with the trip's cities as suggestions; it starts empty for a new entry (owner's request: nothing pre-filled) and is filled with the photo's own town from `whereIs` (or the picked place's town), even when a trip city is close by; on save a name matching a trip city uses that stop, a new name is added with `ensureStop`, and an empty field falls back to the city of that date; the owner can type over it (saved text is placed with `findPlaces` after save), tap 🔎 Find to pick a match, or ✕ to clear.
- City names typed or dictated are matched forgivingly: `cityKey` ignores case, accents, punctuation, spaces and St/Saint; `fuzzyCity` also allows ~1 wrong letter per 6 ("Biaritz", "Saint Jean de Lutz"); `sameCity` is used wherever a form matches its City field to a trip stop. `ensureStop` strips a dictated trailing period, snaps to a trip city first, then asks `geocode` (told the name may be misheard, with the trip's cities as an area hint) and reuses a trip city if Claude's proper name is one ("Donostia" → San Sebastián).
- Place search ("🔎 Find the place" in the activity/meal, expense and stay forms): `findPlaces` asks Claude via `sample` for up to 5 real matches near the trip city (artifacts can't reach map services), and picking one fills the name, address, pin (lat/lon) and nearest trip city. Places and expenses may carry an optional `address` (expenses also lat/lon).
- Share (trip header, or the share icon on a day), in numbered steps: 1 which days (whole trip, today, each day, or from/to; a city chip sets `o.city` so `shareSlice` keeps only that city's stays and activities, no legs, with its days as ranges, e.g. Bordeaux "Oct 2 – 4 & Oct 13 – 14"; stats then show days, things to do, meals), 2 size, 3 title, 4 what shows, 5 photos, plus an "On the share" checklist of the slice's activities/meals (`o.hide`, passed to `shareSlice` as `hide`; their photos drop out too) and "Keep private" (`o.priv`: spots = no activity pins/tracks, video camera stays on the city; names = activities shown by type via `shareName`; times; dates = "N days"), applied to the image, video and caption. Activities follow the Days order (plans last); photos are labelled with their activity and the starting pick is each activity's cover photo in order (up to 4), so a day is covered, not just its first activity. Outside city mode `shareSlice`'s stops are the towns the slice actually touched (the morning town of its first day, legs, activities, expenses, stays), not stored stop date ranges. A one-town slice with photos (no legs, hops or tracks) has no map on the card; the photos take its space. While the 🎬 video records, its button reads "■ Stop · N%" and tapping it cancels and returns to the image. `shareSlice` also returns `hops` (unlogged drives between consecutive done activities in different cities), drawn as car lines with their city pins on the card map; the default title and city list follow that travel order. Then: format (post 4:5, square, story 9:16), title, map/stats/activities/prices, up to 4 photos; drawn on a canvas, saved via `downloads` or the phone's share sheet. 🎬 Video turns the same choices into a moving route (the whole Days list in order: legs traced with the camera following, Strava/GPX tracks, a "moment" card for each activity and Food & drink/Activities/Shopping expense (time · category, name, price only when the Prices chip is on; ratings are kept in the data and shown only in the forms and on the city page, not in Days, the map card or shares) with its picked photo as a polaroid, a drawn car hop (estimated km) between consecutive moments in different cities when no leg connects them (not in city mode), city pins, picked photos popping in, a Day N · km badge, closing stats), recorded live with MediaRecorder at 720 px wide (MP4 on iPhone, WebM fallback) and saved via `downloads`. The share sheet's "✂️ Edit video · photos · music" panel holds: music (none, three built-in tunes synthesised on the device by `makeTune`, or the owner's own audio file with start/volume/fade; mixed in through WebAudio and recorded with the video), pace, the scene list (`plan.scenes`: untick to cut, Short/Normal/Long/Longer per scene, stored in `o.edit`), and per-photo edits (rotate, look, framing in `o.photoFx`) applied by `sharePic`/`fxImage` to the image and video only, never to the stored photo.
- Photos: tapping any photo opens the viewer (swipe, make cover = first in photoIds, replace, delete everywhere). Forms show ✕ / tap-for-cover and apply on Save. The app calls `assets.delete` only for files nothing references any more, after the owner deletes or replaces them.
- Leg order: `tripLegs` sorts by date, then `chainDay` follows the chain of cities within a day (A→B before B→C), with times only breaking ties. `legTime` ignores a leg's time when the day had timed entries in the from-city after it (e.g. a stray 00:37); `dayOrder` uses the chain and slots timed entries between the timed legs around them, by their city. Route shows a "↻ … is in the route twice" card for the same drive logged twice on a day (`dupLegs`); "Merge into one drive" adds the costs (same currency only), joins notes, keeps a time, and deletes the extra.
- Recorded drives: day ＋ → "📍 Drive from GPS file" (`gpxDrive` → `readDrive`: parseGpx, from/to town = trip city within 8 km of the track's ends, else `whereIs`) opens `legSheet` with the recording (real km, start time, duration); a drive already logged that day between the same cities is updated instead of duplicated. The leg form itself has "📍 Load GPS file" / Remove. GPX files under More ways to add that look like car trips (named so, or >40 km/h) go the same way. Web pages can't record GPS in the background, so the owner records with a tracker app (e.g. Open GPX Tracker) and imports the .gpx.
- Google Maps Timeline import (Route header "🗺️ Timeline", or More ways to add): the owner exports Google Maps → Your Timeline → Settings → Export Timeline data (.json). `parseTimeline` reads the on-device/Android shape ({semanticSegments}), the iPhone shape (top-level array, "geo:lat,lon" points, durationMinutesOffsetFromStartTime) and old Takeout ({timelineObjects:[{activitySegment}]}, E7); vehicle/train/bus/ferry/plane segments become drives (`TL_MODE`), with the road from timelinePath points in their time window. `timelineImport` keeps trip dates, joins same-mode segments <30 min apart (fuel/coffee stops), drops <2 km, names ends like `readDrive`, and lists them with checkboxes; a drive already in Route that day between the same towns is updated (track, real km, time, duration), otherwise a new leg is created. Patchy GPS (the owner's first real iPhone export, Oct 9: one 10:46–21:54 drive Google called 24.7 km, path only Burgos→Vitoria then the arrival): a drive's start/end snap to the visit just before/after it (≤20 min) or the path's first point; Google's distance is used only if ≥ 90% of the straight line, else km = max(path length, straight × 1.25) and flagged estimated, with a ⚠️ note in the list; drives whose towns can't be named are counted ("couldn't be placed") instead of silently dropped. When the owner's own legs that day already chain from the drive's start to its end (Burgos → Pamplona → Serres-Castet), the import cuts the road at the point nearest each town in between and adds each part to its leg (keeping the legs' km and times, filling a missing departure/arrival) instead of creating a new leg. A drive on a day whose legs already carry a recorded road from that start (Google re-sends earlier days in every export) is listed unticked as "that day is already recorded". New towns are created with the drive's own coordinates (`ensureStop(..., at)`: used when Claude can't place the name or places it >30 km away; country borrowed from the nearest known stop), and one drive failing no longer aborts the rest (the toast lists what couldn't be added). Visits themselves aren't imported as activities.
- Steps: day ＋ → "👟 Steps" (`stepsSheet`): one box per trip day so far (newest first, the tapped day highlighted) to type counts; where the view can send images to Claude (`sampleImages`>0) a "Read a screenshot" button (Health → Steps → Show All Data, read by `readSteps`) fills the boxes. Saved in `trip.steps`. The owner's Claude iPhone app view reports no image support, so photo reading (steps, receipts, photo names) only works there via Safari at claude.ai; `photoBatch` says so with a toast. Shown as a 👟 tag on the day, in the folded-day summary, and as a total/average above Days. Pages can't read Apple Health directly.
- Route "missing drives" card (`missingLegs`): orders the trip's timed activities/meals/expenses by time, collapses them to a city sequence (drops a lone entry far off the route), and suggests a car leg (estimated km, `kmEstimated`) between consecutive cities that no existing leg already connects that day; when following the day's logged legs from the previous city it only uses legs that had left by the entry's time (else a 14:29 yard sale in Ramous next to Puyoô became "Capbreton → Ramous", Capbreton being where the evening ended), and skips towns <10 km from where you were; the owner ticks and taps "Add to the route". Only dates up to today.
- My world (Airbnb-style, owner's request; countries first, cities on tap): the world map (560 tall, key `world2`) opens fitted to the places you've been (`focus`: home + past stops, Mercator, room left for pins and the zoom buttons; can zoom out to the whole world), tapping a visited country's shape opens its page (`onCountry`), and it has one photo pin per visited country — except countries in `CITY_LEVEL` (owner's call: the US), where every city gets its own pin and stamp that open the city page (`worldUnits`) — (cover from `placeCover` — newest non-private activity photo there — placed at its busiest city, labelled with the country — or the city when only one city of that country was visited (`placeName`: "Miami", not "United States"; same on its stamp, with the country under it) — and the year(s) under it (`yearsTxt`: "2026" or "2023 – 2026"; city pins on a country page too); the pin's point always stays on that city, and a photo that would cover another leans aside on a short stick instead of moving the point). "Where I've been" (`stampRow`) is a scrolling row of country stamps (flag, code, N cities, "Month YYYY" or "N trips"); the stamps, pins and the Countries list open the country page (`R.country` → `renderCountry`): stats, a map fitted to that country's cities (`drawMap` `focus`) with a photo pin per city, city stamps (icon of what was done most, `topCat`) and a city list → city page. `beenCities`/`beenCountries` group stops by city/country across trips (past only).
- Past travels (My world → "＋ Add past trips", `pastTripsSheet`): the owner types or dictates a list ("Rome 2017 and 2022, Cancún Oct 2025"); `readPastTrips` asks Claude for one trip per journey with cities and dates (a year only → Jul 1–7, a month → 1st–7th, a season → its middle month; flagged approx); the owner checks/edits each card (name, cities separated by ;, dates) and saves: a trip (`past:true`) plus a stop per city via `ensureStop`. Photos can then be added from that trip's page.
- Artifacts can't embed other sites (no map tiles, no iframes); the map is drawn with d3 from the inlined world data.
