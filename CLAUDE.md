# Waymark — notes for Claude

Waymark is a travel-log web app published as a claude.ai artifact. The owner (Olivier) is beta-testing it on a trip and may ask for changes from a phone.

## Where things live
- **Live app (source of truth):** https://claude.ai/artifact/VoEoHtepSYciZXkd8Uvspo
- **Business plan page:** https://claude.ai/artifact/T3Ut4zHmKMGTgJujHvNcjo
- `src/app.template.html` — app source. `__WORLD__` is replaced by `src/countries-50m.json` (world-atlas 50m TopoJSON, sharp enough for coastlines at city zoom) at build time.
- `src/build.py` — `python3 src/build.py` writes `waymark.html` at the repo root, the file that gets published.
- `src/playbook.html` — source of the business plan page.
- `src/video-test.html` — one-off phone test for recording the route as video (published separately: https://claude.ai/artifact/XyaNs7QxB6NUPxNxx8KP5v, capability `downloads`).

## How to change the app
1. **Read the live artifact first** (Artifact tool, `action: "read"`, the URL above). If it differs from `src/app.template.html`, the live version wins; bring the repo up to date before editing.
2. Edit `src/app.template.html`, run `python3 src/build.py`.
3. Republish **to the same URL**: Artifact tool, `file_path: waymark.html`, `url: https://claude.ai/artifact/VoEoHtepSYciZXkd8Uvspo`. Omit `capabilities` so the stored ones carry over (db, assets, sample, downloads, mcp/Strava). Omit `icon`.
4. Commit the change to this repo with a short message.

## Never
- Don't write to, reset or reseed the database unless the owner asks. Trips, places, photos and expenses are real data.
- Don't delete uploaded photos (assets).
- Don't change the artifact's sharing or capabilities without asking.

## Data model (artifact `db` collections)
- `trips`: name, startDate, endDate, currency, notes, people [{name, email, phone}] ("Who's going" in the trip form; names feed "Paid by"; contacts never go on a share)
- `stops` (cities): tripId, city, country, countryA2, countryN3, lat, lon, arrive, depart, home (bool, for the home city), photoIds[]
- `legs`: tripId, mode (plane|car|train|bus|ferry|walk), fromId, toId, date, time (optional HH:MM), km, kmEstimated, cost, currency, notes
- `places` (shown as "activities"; meals are places too): tripId, stopId, name, category (meals: breakfast|lunch|dinner|coffee|drinks, picked from the time/name by `mealFor`; things to do: museum, attraction, hike, run, walk, ride, hotel, nightlife, shopping, other; old food|restaurant still read as meals), date, time (optional HH:MM), private (bool: "🔒 Keep off shares", starts unticked on every share), status (want|done), rating 0–5, cost, currency, notes, photoIds[], lat, lon (pin on the trip map; from photo GPS or Claude); imported runs/rides/hikes also carry track [[lat,lon],…] (≤400 pts), distanceKm, movingS, elevM, sport, stravaId
- `expenses`: tripId, label, amount, currency, category, date, time (optional HH:MM), city, stopId (set when city matches a trip stop), paidBy, notes, photoIds[] (receipt photos; "Scan" reads them with `sample` images)
- `stays` (nights): tripId, stopId, city, name, kind (hotel|airbnb|hostel|camping|friends|other), checkIn, checkOut (nights = days between), nights, cost (total, optional), currency, pricePerNight, platform (Booking.com|Airbnb|Expedia|Hotels.com|Vrbo|Hostelworld|Direct|Other|''), bookingRef, address, lat, lon, rating 0–5, notes, photoIds[]. Shown at the end of each night's day in Days ("🛏 Where did you sleep?" when a night has none), on the city page, as H pins on the map, and under Lodging in the budget. Fields are kept structured so stays (and later restaurants/activities) can be analysed across trips.
- Photos are artifact assets, shown via `/_blob/<id>`.
- Icons: `CAT_ICON` (activity/meal types), `MODE_EMOJI` (legs: ✈️ 🚗 🚆 🚌 ⛴️ 🚶) and `EXP_ICON` (expense categories; Transport/Other expenses get a specific icon from their label via `RIDE_ICONS`: flight, train, taxi, fuel, toll, parking, bus, ferry, bike, car) are emoji used in Days, the city page, the budget, the share image and video cards. The budget groups meal costs under "Meals" and splits transport so a flight reads as a flight (`LEG_CAT` for leg costs, `RIDE_CAT` for Transport expenses: Flights, Trains, Car, Taxis, Fuel, Tolls, Parking, Buses, Boats). The trip map shows a mode badge (✈️ 🚗 …) on each leg; a flight to/from home gets it ~12 km from the trip end so it's on screen. A Food & drink expense can be turned into a meal from its sheet ("Make it a meal" creates the place, moves the photos, deletes the expense); a restaurant receipt read from a photo becomes a meal. Each day has a 🍽️ quick-add for meals.

## Runtime
- Plain HTML/JS, no build tooling beyond `build.py`. d3 7.9.0 and topojson 3.0.2 load from cdnjs.
- Capabilities used: `db` (data), `assets` (photos), `sample` (Claude for city/activity coordinates, reading photos and receipts, Top 10), `downloads` (share image), `mcp` (the owner's Strava connector, read-only tools `list_activities` and `get_activity_streams`; added at the owner's request).
- Strava import (Days → From Strava): reads tool input schemas at runtime with `describeTool`, falls back to Claude to normalize an unfamiliar payload. GPX import (Days → GPX file) is parsed in the page.
- When republishing with `capabilities`, pass the full set: {db:{}, assets:{}, sample:{}, downloads:{}, mcp:{servers:[{server:"Strava", tools:["list_activities","get_activity_streams"]}]}}. Omitting `capabilities` keeps the stored ones, which is the normal case.
- Trip page: zoomable map (pinch with two fingers or +/−, tap a pin, ▶ replays the route), then a Days timeline. "From photo" reads a picture's EXIF date/GPS and asks Claude what it shows; a receipt becomes an expense, anything else an activity.
- Days order (dayOrder): a manual order wins once set (press and hold ⠿ for ~0.35 s, then drag; a quick swipe over the handle just scrolls. Dragging writes `dayPos` 10,20,… on that day's legs/places/expenses; changing an item's date clears it; unpositioned items slot in after their automatic neighbour). Otherwise: times win when known; untimed items go with their city between the legs (after the leg arriving there, before the leg leaving); items with no city go last. Time is pre-filled from photo EXIF/receipt, Strava/GPX start, or now when logging on the same day. Every Days line has a time slot (the time, or "+ time") that opens the phone's time wheel and saves `time` directly.
- Expenses have the same Location field via `locFieldHtml`/`bindLoc` (receipt photo GPS → pin + `whereIs` text; saved as address/lat/lon); stays fill the address and pin from a photo's GPS when empty. The main "From photo" flow carries the GPS into whichever form it opens.
- Activity/meal Location field (`pLoc`, saved as `address`): a photo's EXIF GPS pins it and `whereIs` asks Claude what's there to fill the text; the owner can type over it (saved text is placed with `findPlaces` after save), tap 🔎 Find to pick a match, or ✕ to clear.
- Place search ("🔎 Find the place" in the activity/meal, expense and stay forms): `findPlaces` asks Claude via `sample` for up to 5 real matches near the trip city (artifacts can't reach map services), and picking one fills the name, address, pin (lat/lon) and nearest trip city. Places and expenses may carry an optional `address` (expenses also lat/lon).
- Share (trip header, or the share icon on a day), in numbered steps: 1 which days (whole trip, today, each day, or from/to; a city chip sets `o.city` so `shareSlice` keeps only that city's stays and activities, no legs, with its days as ranges, e.g. Bordeaux "Oct 2 – 4 & Oct 13 – 14"; stats then show days, things to do, meals), 2 size, 3 title, 4 what shows, 5 photos, plus an "On the share" checklist of the slice's activities/meals (`o.hide`, passed to `shareSlice` as `hide`; their photos drop out too) and "Keep private" (`o.priv`: spots = no activity pins/tracks, video camera stays on the city; names = activities shown by type via `shareName`; times; dates = "N days"), applied to the image, video and caption. Activities follow the Days order (plans last); photos are labelled with their activity and the starting pick is each activity's cover photo in order (up to 4), so a day is covered, not just its first activity. Then: format (post 4:5, square, story 9:16), title, map/stats/activities/prices, up to 4 photos; drawn on a canvas, saved via `downloads` or the phone's share sheet. 🎬 Video turns the same choices into a moving route (the whole Days list in order: legs traced with the camera following, Strava/GPX tracks, a "moment" card for each activity and Food & drink/Activities/Shopping expense (time · category, name, price only when the Prices chip is on; ratings are kept in the data and shown only in the forms and on the city page, not in Days, the map card or shares) with its picked photo as a polaroid, city pins, picked photos popping in, a Day N · km badge, closing stats), recorded live with MediaRecorder at 720 px wide (MP4 on iPhone, WebM fallback) and saved via `downloads`. The share sheet's "✂️ Edit video · photos · music" panel holds: music (none, three built-in tunes synthesised on the device by `makeTune`, or the owner's own audio file with start/volume/fade; mixed in through WebAudio and recorded with the video), pace, the scene list (`plan.scenes`: untick to cut, Short/Normal/Long/Longer per scene, stored in `o.edit`), and per-photo edits (rotate, look, framing in `o.photoFx`) applied by `sharePic`/`fxImage` to the image and video only, never to the stored photo.
- Photos: tapping any photo opens the viewer (swipe, make cover = first in photoIds, replace, delete everywhere). Forms show ✕ / tap-for-cover and apply on Save. The app calls `assets.delete` only for files nothing references any more, after the owner deletes or replaces them.
- Artifacts can't embed other sites (no map tiles, no iframes); the map is drawn with d3 from the inlined world data.
